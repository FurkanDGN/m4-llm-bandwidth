In 1642, Blaise Pascal, a French mathematician, created the Pascaline, a mechanical calculator that could perform addition and subtraction. This invention was a significant step towards automating arithmetic operations. Later, in 1673, Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz, a German mathematician, improved upon Pascal's design by creating the Stepped Reckoner, which could perform multiplication and division.
